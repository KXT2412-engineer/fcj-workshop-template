Here is the list and introduction of the blogs that our team has published on the [AWS Study Group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/awsstudygroupfcj)

### [Blog 1 - HOW DID AWS UPGRADE AMAZON COGNITO SO THAT USERS BARELY NOTICED?](3.1-Blog1/)

This blog delves into how AWS executed a large-scale infrastructure "migration" campaign for Amazon Cognito with a Zero Downtime goal. Instead of just discussing new features, the article brings valuable lessons in system development mindset (such as Shadow mode, Dual-write, Anti-entropy) to safely migrate hundreds of millions of user profiles.

### [Blog 2 - AWS KNOWLEDGE & CLOUD-NATIVE MINDSET FROM A PRACTICAL INTERNSHIP](3.2-Blog2/)

This blog shares valuable practical experiences from an internship, focusing on applying the system decoupling mindset with Amazon S3. The article also guides how to manage access permissions with AWS IAM, optimize storage costs, secure credentials, and solve the problem of sharing data safely through Pre-signed URLs.

### [Blog 3 - SCALABLE E-COMMERCE WEBSITE ARCHITECTURE ON AWS](3.3-Blog3/)

This blog introduces a standard infrastructure architecture on AWS specifically for e-commerce websites with frequently fluctuating traffic. By flexibly combining services such as Route 53, CloudFront, ALB, ECS Fargate, and Aurora Serverless, the system ensures fast response times, high security, database load reduction, and easy auto-scaling during peak seasons.
